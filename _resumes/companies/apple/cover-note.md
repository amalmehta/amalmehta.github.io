# Cover note: Apple, Applied Research Engineer

I build learning systems end to end, then ship them where people can use them. AlphaGo Lite is the clearest example: an AlphaZero-style 9×9 Go engine I trained from scratch on one laptop CPU. It went from losing all 40 games against GNU Go's weakest level to winning 32. It runs in the browser through ONNX and in a native Mac app through Core ML.

In post-training, SFT then GRPO lifted a small transformer policy's held-out success from 70% to 80% over three seeds. Before that, I spent four years at PhysicsAI building generative and RL pipelines for synthetic training data, and did melanoma segmentation research at Berkeley that was published at SPIE Medical Imaging.

Apple runs models on devices people carry every day, under tight compute limits. That's the constraint I've been building for.
