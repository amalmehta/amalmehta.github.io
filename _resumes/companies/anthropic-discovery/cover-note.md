# Cover note: Anthropic, Research Scientist, Discovery (Engineering & Applied Sciences)

Posting: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5468937008 (ML researcher track)

Discovery's work runs on environments, rewards and verifiers that hold up under training pressure, and that's what my research studies. I built a code-repair environment with visible and hidden checks, then trained on the visible ones: pass rate rose from 35% to 58% while true correctness fell from 31% to 2%, because the agent learned to delete untested branches. Across 50 follow-ups, each with its prediction written down first, choosing which episodes to audit held false success to 4%, against 24% for random audits on the same budget.

I've also built simulation-based environments and checked them against the real world: a quadrotor simulator modelled on PX4 hardware, a quadruped kicking policy trained in IsaacGym and run on a Unitree Go1, and, at PhysicsAI, an RL loop whose reward was a detector's accuracy on real drone imagery.

My experiments are small. I want to work on the same questions where the tasks are real engineering and the results feed production training.
